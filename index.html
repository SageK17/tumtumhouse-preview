About TumTum House

Learn. Build. Grow.

TumTum House started with a simple idea: a place where useful digital products, inspiration and resources come together.

From self-development and organisation to online business, creativity and lifestyle, we are building a collection of products designed to help you learn, create and move forward. We are still growing, and the shop keeps expanding with new digital products and resources.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

How do I receive a digital product? +
Every product is a digital file. After you order in the webshop you download it, open it on your phone, tablet or computer, and print it or edit the text in Canva if you like.
How do the posters work? +
A poster is a digital file for € 1,00 for your own use; the webshop also offers a resale licence for € 5,00 extra. Print it at home, at a copy shop or through a print service, as a poster, a framed print, a cushion cover or a canvas. The poster studio shows you how it will look.
Where does my money go? +
To the product, and partly to charity. A part of every order is donated.
Something wrong with my file? +
Email TumTum.House26@gmail.com and we will help. For returns and refunds, the conditions of the webshop apply.
Where is TumTum House based? +
In Overijssel, the Netherlands. The products are digital, so you can order from anywhere.
